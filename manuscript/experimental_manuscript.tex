\begin{abstract}
Some weights in theta cycles modulo $p^2$ are still unknown.  We study the
next unresolved wedge by exact computation.  In $692$ ordinary cross-wedge
cells, one shifted quadratic matches all $458$ interior obstructions.  The
$234$ disagreements lie on three affine boundary lines.  Two separate tests
give $2{,}324$ exact matches with one factorial boundary scalar.  We also
prove that the obstruction is a first Hasse jet.  The relevant Hasse
quotients have a perfect pairing, and theta is skew-adjoint for this pairing.
We give the minimal-weight projection and prove that exact filtration
separates the boundary directions.  These theorems reduce the proposed
shifted-conic law to one factorial block identity and two endpoint
identities.  Those three identities remain conjectures.  The paper includes
code, negative controls, and exact tests that can detect a failure.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} theta cycles; modular forms modulo prime powers;
experimental mathematics; Hasse invariant; Bockstein class; weight
filtration.

\noindent\textbf{2020 Mathematics Subject Classification:}
11F33, 11F11, 11Y40.

\section{Introduction}

The theta cycle of a modular form modulo a prime is classical.  Modulo
$p^2$, weight lowering has a new first-order obstruction.  A form may lower
in weight modulo $p$ but fail to lower in the same way modulo $p^2$.
Ahlgren, Raum, and Richter determined the initial segment and many later
parts of the cycle \cite{ARR2026}.  Some low points in the next band remain
unknown for general primes.

The basic filtration theory modulo $p$ comes from work of
Swinnerton-Dyer and Serre \cite{SwDyer1973,Serre1973}.  Katz gave a
geometric theory of modular forms in characteristic $p$, including the
Hasse invariant and the theta operator \cite{Katz1973}.  Later work on
modular weights and companion forms developed this viewpoint further
\cite{Edixhoven1992,ColemanVoloch1992}.

We ask one exact question.  Does the quadratic pattern in the resolved wedge
remain valid after reflection into the next wedge?  The first candidate
failed on every tested $D=0$ cell.  We then added the missing minimal-weight
projection.  In the interior, the lower Bockstein class was controlled by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-shifted-conic-experimental}
 i'^2+(k-1)i'-(n+1)(n+2)\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_p.
\end{equation}
All disagreements occurred on three affine lines.  Their full residuals
then suggested one factorial block and two triangular endpoint formulas.

The paper explains how we found and tested this formula.  The calculation
uses exact modular arithmetic and Sturm-safe remainders.  It includes a
failed baseline, a blind prime extension, and a separate test in a
two-dimensional cusp-form space.  We then prove the geometry used by the
experiment.  The weight-lowering obstruction is a first Hasse jet.  The
ambient quotient has a perfect pairing, and theta is skew-adjoint.
Multiplication by the integral Hasse inclusion commutes with theta modulo
$p^2$.  We also give the minimal-weight projection.  Exact filtration
separates the proposed boundary directions.

Three identities remain open.  The first-Hasse-jet formula should reduce to
a factorial block identity.  This identity would give the generic
shifted-conic law and the observed scalar on the third line.  Two endpoint
coefficients remain open on the other boundary lines.  We state all three
claims as conjectures because the present evidence is finite.

The experiments identify the classification problem and the three boundary
cases.  The theorems explain why the candidate has this form.  They also
reduce the open part to explicit identities.

\subsection{Main results and limits}

The paper has three kinds of results.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Uniform theorems.}  We prove the first-Hasse-jet description,
      perfect Hasse-quotient duality, theta skew-adjointness, the explicit
      minimal-weight projection, and separation of the boundary directions.
\item \textbf{Exact finite results.}  The main census has $692$ cells.  Two
      later tests give $2{,}324$ exact matches with the proposed third-line
      scalar.  A separate index audit checks $210{,}176$ parameter cells
      through $p=101$.
\item \textbf{Open claims.}  One factorial block identity and two endpoint
      identities are still conjectural.  The full shifted-conic law is
      conditional on these identities.
\end{enumerate}
This split is used throughout the paper.  No finite calculation is stated as
a theorem for all primes.

\subsection{Relation to prior work}

Ahlgren--Raum--Richter \cite{ARR2026} prove the initial segment, further
exact values or bounds modulo $p^2$, and a quadratic condition for the
exceptional positions in their resolved region.  Our code also checks the
resolved $D=2$ subwedge, whose conic is
\[
 i'^2+(k-1)i'-n(n+1)=0.
\]
We study the complementary $D=0$ subwedge.  Reflection changes the constant
term to $(n+1)(n+2)$.  A new first-Hasse-jet projection is needed before this
expression gives the actual obstruction.  This is not a restatement of
Ahlgren--Raum--Richter.  The geometric pairing alone also does not prove the
shifted-conic law.

\section{Remaining symbolic identities}

Theorem~\ref{thm:explicit-periodic-first-jet} constructs the minimal
mod-$p$ representative in characteristic zero and computes its normal
coefficient along the Hasse divisor.  A complete cross-wedge theorem now
requires the factorial block reduction in
Conjecture~\ref{conj:factorial-block-reduction} and the two triangular
associated-graded endpoint coefficients.  The residual directions and their
independence are already proved.  The open task is to simplify the explicit
operator formula in the direct block basis.

On the third line, that simplification predicts
\[
 \mathcal B_i-\mathcal L_i
 =-m(k-2m-2)!
 X^{k-2m-1}\theta^{p-k}f.
\]
The displayed block is nonzero by
Proposition~\ref{prop:l3-block-nonzero}, and the scalar is a unit by
Proposition~\ref{prop:l3-closed-scalar-unit}.  Thus the unresolved point is
not nonvanishing, but identification of the computed scalar with the formal
block reduction for every admissible parameter set.

\section{Conclusion}

The exact experiments find a shifted quadratic in the interior of the next
theta-cycle wedge.  They also find three boundary residuals.  The geometry
and filtration results place these residuals in the predicted quotient
directions.  The first-Hasse-jet formula reduces the remaining problem to
three coefficient identities.  The factorial block reduction and the two
triangular endpoint identities remain open.  The table below separates the
uniform theorems, finite results, conditional claims, and conjectures.
